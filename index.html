The argument so far

Two moves on the board now, and the second one is the one that starts to break the statement open:

1. Political liberation was one focus of Black Consciousness, because after 1960 black South Africans had no legal party, no vote and no citizenship.

2. But the movement's starting diagnosis was psychological, not political: Biko argued that liberation had to begin in the mind of the oppressed.

A marker will still ask what the movement actually did about it. Lesson 3 is the answer.
